# 1. Core conclusion

## Creative Fit and Production Feasibility are separate

`Creative Fit` answers:

> What art direction / persuasion presentation is appropriate for this business and its customers?

`Production Feasibility` answers:

> How can that selected direction be realized safely with the assets/evidence currently available?

Therefore the following must **not** select Creative Family:

- photo asset quantity
- evidence quantity
- scraping difficulty
- source coverage
- staff/facility photo availability
- rights clarity
- pricing completeness
- review availability

These can affect realization and fallback after Creative Fit is decided; they do not define what creative is appropriate.

# 4. Creative Family discovery

Eight evidence-grounded family hypotheses are preserved:

1. **Sensory Sanctuary / Atmospheric Rest**
2. **Clinical Calm / Structured Reassurance**
3. **Editorial High-Consideration / Aspirational Authority**
4. **Human Craft / Provenance-Led Intimacy**
5. **Guided Choice / Diagnostic Clarity**
6. **Proof-Led Process Authority**
7. **Local Human / Community Warmth**
8. **Category Education / Open Discovery**

These are **not color/style presets**.

A Family represents a repeated relationship among:

- customer state
- desired authority
- media role
- information density
- trust logic
- composition
- conversion commitment

Examples of evidence linkage:

# 5. Beauty / Wellness taxonomy conclusion

For Beauty/Wellness, strong variation is not adequately captured by labels such as:

- luxury
- natural
- clinic
- feminine
- minimal

The research instead supports family distinctions based on **what deserves visual authority and why**.

Examples:

- a relaxation/experience service may need sensory atmosphere
- a trust-sensitive treatment may need clinical calm and boundaries
- a multi-service salon may need guided choice
- an owner-led specialist may need human/provenance authority
- an unfamiliar healing/wellness category may need category education before conversion

The taxonomy is a **proposal**, not a final production family list. Multi-company validation remains required.

# 7. Compatibility and prohibited combinations

Key hypothesis examples:

### Compatible

- high sensory weight + low immediate information burden -> atmospheric/sensory family can lead
- high trust requirement + complex conditions -> clinical/process authority should dominate proof scenes
- multiple services + choice anxiety -> guided choice becomes material
- owner/staff importance high -> human relationship can become primary authority if safely realizable
- unfamiliar category + low readiness -> education/permission before high commitment

### Prohibited or high-risk

- clinical calm + decorative luxury media dominating evidence scenes
- sensory sanctuary + generic spa stock presented like company reality
- guided choice + equal-card grid repeated throughout the whole LP
- human/local family + unverifiable staff/premises/process claims
- premium editorial Hero + generic FAQ/CTA tail
- proof/process family + feature inventory without customer task/risk framing

# 8. Template Resemblance Model v0

The completed research supports treating Template Resemblance as a **hard premium concern**.

## Candidate comparison dimensions

- Hero silhouette / composition
- section topology
- repeated module sequence
- dominant grid / alignment behavior
- typography hierarchy
- color topology
- media framing / crop / masks
- whitespace / density rhythm
- material / decorative grammar
- CTA choreography
- motion pattern when material
- screenshot-level gestalt

## What does NOT prove uniqueness

- color swap
- photo swap
- logo swap
- copy swap

## Candidate hard fails

- unrelated businesses look like the same Hero/service/CTA shell
- late-page rhythm remains identical despite different customer decisions
- identical media mask/crop/type hierarchy makes screenshots feel derivative
- multimodal Human Reality review repeatedly says “same template”

### Important calibration limit

The recovered research package contains same-family-but-distinct and clearly-different-family seed pairs, but a dedicated saved set of **same-template-looking labeled pairs** was not recovered.

Therefore:

**No fake numeric threshold is proposed.**

Before Production uses an automated hard numeric gate, generated-output and benchmark pair judgments must be added and calibrated.
