# Hearing Schema & Evidence Completion Loop

## Purpose

`HEARING_REQUIRED` is a controlled route for collecting only the facts needed
to complete a Production LP. It is not a general customer questionnaire and it
does not create customer-facing copy.

## Contract

`HEARING_REQUIRED → Hearing Field → natural customer question → customer answer
→ unverified Evidence Candidate → provenance / verification / rights → Safety
recheck → PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE or HOLD`

The planner uses `NEED AND MISSING AND RELEVANT`. Existing eligible Evidence
suppresses the corresponding question. Fields sharing an answerable meaning
unit are deduplicated, so a single process question can cover both process and
next-step requirements when the answer genuinely contains both facts.

## Responsibility boundary

- Hearing Planner: selects fields, questions, priority, verification follow-up,
  and completion criteria.
- Evidence Completion: converts an answer into an unverified candidate and
  attaches provenance/verification/rights metadata.
- Safety Layer: makes the final Production eligibility decision.
- Creative Layer: decides presentation only after approved Evidence exists.

An answer is never a Production claim by itself. Objective claims such as
`No.1`, satisfaction, qualification, or certification require the appropriate
document or third-party source. A photo or logo requires explicit usage rights;
being present on a website is not permission to reuse it.

## Requirement classes and blocking

- `UNIVERSAL_REQUIRED`: shared operating facts such as responsible party,
  process, scope, and post-click flow.
- `DOMAIN_REQUIRED`: facts whose need depends on domain and goal, such as fee,
  ability proof, or aftercare.
- `CONDITIONAL_REQUIRED`: policy facts triggered by a relevant risk, decision,
  response, or rights objection.
- `HIGH_VALUE` / `OPTIONAL`: enrichment only; they do not rescue an incomplete
  Sales Sample.

`P0` is page-blocking, `P1` is claim/section-blocking, `P2` is high-value,
and `P3` is optional. Current field definitions use `CLAIM_BLOCKING`,
`SECTION_BLOCKING`, or `NON_BLOCKING` explicitly; no field is silently promoted
to a stronger level by the planner.

## Answer statuses

`ANSWERED` is not the same as `COMPLETE`. Candidates begin as
`UNVERIFIED_CUSTOMER_INPUT`. Depending on the field they can move to
`NEEDS_VERIFICATION`, `NEEDS_DOCUMENT`, `NEEDS_RIGHTS`, `CONFLICT`, or
`COMPLETE`. `COMPLETE` still returns to the Safety selector and must satisfy
the Production usage, provenance, verification, and rights gates.

## Five-case dry run

The deterministic fixture is `data/hearing_dry_run_v1.json`, generated by
`scripts/run_hearing_dry_run.py`. It covers P02, P09, P10, 森人, and こころ整体院
with synthetic Safety gaps. It is test/research output, not customer evidence.

## Not implemented

No Hearing UI, form, LINE bot, email sender, or external customer submission
path is implemented in this phase.
