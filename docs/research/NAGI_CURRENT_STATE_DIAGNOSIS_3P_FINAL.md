# Nagi Current-State Diagnosis — Round 3P

Scope: diagnosis only. This document does not write final Nagi copy and does not invent practitioner, process, duration, scope, healing, qualification, price, or outcome facts.

## Design diagnosis

| Area | Current issue | Quality-ceiling implication |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Visual direction and page structure do not yet create enough authored authority | Establish one customer-state-led opening and one memorable visual cause |
| Background / atmosphere | Background treatment is not consistently carrying mood, transition, or persuasion | Background must alter customer state, not only decorate a section |
| Media | Evidence and desire are not yet strong enough to make the service feel real | Use verified client evidence when available; until then use intentional abstraction, not fake owner imagery |
| Typography | Some hierarchy choices look designed, but surface language remains generic | Typography must support meaning units and natural Japanese line shape |
| Late page | Trust, FAQ, contact, and ending lose authority relative to the Hero | Preserve art direction and emotional arc through proof, objection, and final action |
| Mobile | A desktop composition cannot simply collapse into a narrow column | Re-art-direct order, crop, pacing, and CTA proximity per viewport |

## Copy diagnosis

- Hero risks exposing internal IA vocabulary instead of naming a customer moment.
- Receive / Learn / Healing can become service labels or abstract reassurance.
- Trust must show verified boundary/process; “寄り添う” alone is not evidence.
- Action must reduce the next decision without inventing a low-pressure policy.
- Ending must provide final conviction and a specific next event rather than a generic form.

## Acceptance implication

The next Rin rebuild must be evaluated against the current pixels and copy as a full-page experience. Local improvements are insufficient if Hero, late-page, mobile, or preserved strengths regress.
